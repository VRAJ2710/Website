GOLD & MACRO INTELLIGENCE

Gold Desk: structure, levels, and macro context

Gold sits at the intersection of rates, the dollar, liquidity, and risk. The Gold Desk turns those forces into a readable framework for tracking structure and testing a thesis.

Open the Weekly PlaybookDaily Dispatch BriefMarket SignalsNewsroom

What moves gold

Gold can respond to real yields, dollar trends, central-bank demand, liquidity, and shifts in risk appetite. No single driver explains every session, so the desk keeps the broader regime in view.

Structure before a forecast

Trend, range, momentum, and failed breaks are evidence about what the market is doing now. They are not promises about what it must do next.

A repeatable weekly process

Start with the macro backdrop, mark the important levels, identify the catalyst calendar, and write down what would change your mind. That process is more durable than a fixed target.
